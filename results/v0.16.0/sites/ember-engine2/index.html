What comes off the fire

Six things we cook every service

The menu changes with the market every week. These are the parts that do not change, because they are what people book for.

Whole sea bream over oak

Scored, rubbed with lemon and thyme, and finished over the grate. Served whole at the table with salsa verde and a knife for each guest.

Aged lamb shoulder

Twenty-eight days in the cold room, then six hours over apple wood with rosemary and garlic. It comes off the bone at the first touch of a fork.

Sourdough and rye, baked daily

Two starters, mixed the night before and baked from six every morning. The rye is dense, dark and sour, and it is sold until it runs out.

A short natural-wine list

Forty bottles from growers we have visited, most of them poured by the glass. Ask for the carafe of the week, which is always a surprise.

The 24-seat private room

A room with its own door and a single long oak table. The chef talks you through each course from the pass, which is a few steps away.

Smoked-apple negroni

Bristol gin, sweet vermouth, and a rinse of apple-wood smoke under a glass dome. Made to order, and the apple slice is cut from the morning's fruit.

How a visit works

Three steps from the door to the fire

  1. 01

    Book, or walk in at the bar

    Reserve through the button above, or call the restaurant on 0117 555 0142. We keep a third of the tables free for walk-ins until seven on Fridays and Saturdays.

  2. 02

    Start with the bread

    A loaf of sourdough and a piece of rye arrive warm with cultured butter while you read the menu. Ask for a second rye if the first one disappears.

  3. 03

    Share what comes off the grate

    Dishes come when they are ready, not in a fixed order. Whole fish and lamb are carved at the table, and the kitchen will tell you what is best that night.
