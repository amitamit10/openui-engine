What we design

Three kinds of project, one method of working.

Every commission starts with a measured survey and a written brief. The same structural and energy checks run on a barn, a school or a single house.

  • Fabric-first low-energy homes

    Wall build-ups are specified to the millimetre, with airtightness tested before plaster goes on. Typical new homes come in at 60% below the 2021 Part L baseline.

  • A fixed-fee design stage

    The fee for concept, design development and planning is agreed in writing before we start. No hourly timesheets and no surprise variations for the drawings we were always going to make.

  • In-house structural engineering

    Our engineer sits in the studio, not on a sub-consultant's invoice. Steel, glulam and CLT spans are checked during the sketch stage, so the plan survives its first contact with a beam.

  • School extensions around term time

    Phasing is planned against the academic calendar. Structural works are kept to the summer holiday, and noisy work is sequenced away from exam weeks. Trusts get a phasing plan before they get a price.

  • Barn conversions that keep the frame

    We survey the timber first and design around it. Original frames, threshing-floor levels and stone cills stay where they are, and new work is set back so the building still reads as a barn.

  • Daylight and overheating modelling

    Every south glazing ratio is modelled against summer temperatures for Leeds, not a generic year. You see the overheating risk and the heating load side by side before you choose a window size.

How a project runs

Three stages, each with a written sign-off.

  1. 01

    Visit and brief

    Weeks 1 to 2

    We walk the site with you, measure it, and talk through how the building will be used on a Tuesday in February. You receive a one-page brief and a feasibility note with a fixed price for the design stage.

  2. 02

    Design stage

    Weeks 3 to 9

    Two options are drawn, energy-modelled and structurally checked. You choose one at a review meeting, and we develop it to a planning-ready set with a design and access statement.

  3. 03

    Planning submission

    Week 11

    We submit to the local authority and answer its first round of questions. Full service clients continue into tender, site inspections and handover, with the same team throughout.

Questions

Before you call us.

Yes. The fee is agreed in writing after the site visit and does not change if we need extra drawings to satisfy the planning officer. The only additions are instructed changes to the brief, which we price before we start them.

Part L is the 2021 energy standard for new homes. A house 60% below it uses roughly six-tenths of the energy a compliant house would, which for a typical three-bedroom home is in the region of a few hundred pounds a year at current tariffs. We model this with your own occupancy and tariff.

We design during term time and build during the holidays. Structural works and roof connections are scheduled into the summer break, and the trust approves the phasing plan before tender.

Most of our work is within 30 miles of Leeds, in Wakefield, Bradford, Harrogate and the Aire and Calder valleys. We take projects further afield when the client is already working with us and the site can be visited in a day.

Design stage includes the planning submission and the design and access statement. Full service adds building regulations drawings and the specification notes an approved inspector needs. We do not act as the approved inspector ourselves.

For most home extensions and barn conversions you can stay in the building. For a full rebuild or a structural change to the main house, we will say so at the feasibility stage and plan the temporary accommodation with you.
