What you get

Everything a good class needs, and nothing it doesn't.

No slide decks read aloud, no forty-person webinars. Six learners, a teacher who listens, and a week built around actually talking.

  • Classes of six, never sixty

    Every live class is capped at six adults at the same level. You will speak in every lesson, and your teacher will remember how you said your last sentence.

  • A speaking club every Saturday

    Ninety minutes of small-talk, debates about the weather and very silly role-play, in groups of up to twelve. Drop in whenever your week allows.

  • Progress reports a human wrote

    Each term your teacher writes you a report in plain English: what you can now do, what still trips you up, and what to practise before next time. No algorithm, no star ratings.

  • Pronunciation you can see

    Record a sentence, then overlay it on your teacher's. The Polish consonant pile-ups and the Portuguese nasal vowels stop being mysteries and start being a habit.

  • Role-play for real counters

    Ticket offices, GP appointments, the landlord who wants the deposit back. We rehearse the awkward conversation in class so Thursday's version goes better.

  • Timetables that bend

    Classes run at 07:30, 12:15, 19:00 and on Saturdays. Pick a slot, switch once a term at no cost, and keep your place in the group if the shift changes.

How learning works

Three steps from hello to a sentence you mean.

  1. 01

    Take a free trial lesson

    Twenty-five minutes with a teacher, not a bot. They will find your level, tell you honestly if you need a lower class, and recommend the tier that fits your move.

  2. 02

    Join your class on a fixed start date

    Courses start every six weeks at A1 to C1. You get the same six classmates for the whole term, a shared group chat and a teacher who learns how you learn.

  3. 03

    Speak in week one, report in week twelve

    Your first lesson is spent talking, not reading. At the end of the term, you get a written report and a clear next goal, and you start again from there.
