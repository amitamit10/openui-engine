The programme

Six strands, one harbour, two working days.

Every strand is built around field results, not vendor pitches. Talks run 25 minutes with ten for questions, and most speakers bring data to share.

  • Underwater robotics

    Field-tested AUVs and ROVs with the telemetry to prove it. Fourteen talks on navigation, docking and sensor payloads.

  • Seaweed farming

    Line design, seeding and harvest logistics, from growers in Scotland, Norway and Chile. Bring your site questions.

  • Coastal sensing

    Buoys, tide gauges and HF radar feeding real harbour and coastal-authority decisions, including what went wrong.

  • Marine data

    Pipelines for sensor fleets, open datasets and the data-quality checks that stop a bad week of readings reaching a report.

  • Six hands-on workshops

    Build a sonar processing pipeline in Python, or draft a seaweed site permit application with a regulator on the panel. 24 seats each.

  • Harbour boat session

    Two 90-minute sails on a research vessel from Aberdeen harbour, with sensors live on deck. 30 places per sail.

How attending works

Three steps from booking to the boat.

  1. 01

  3. 03

    Arrive at the harbour

    Badges are collected at the Aberdeen harbour registration desk from 08:00. Boat sessions leave from pontoon C at 13:00 and 15:30.
