What we do

Everything a worried owner needs, under one roof.

Most of what we do happens in a single visit, so you are not sent across town for a blood test or a second opinion.

  • 09:1009:4010:5011:2014:3016:20

    Same-day appointments

    We hold twelve slots a day for problems that cannot wait, so a limping dog or a cat off its food is seen the same afternoon.

  • A cat-only waiting room

    Cats wait behind a closed door with raised perches and no dogs. Ask for the cat room when you book and we will keep your cat away from the barking.

  • In-house lab results

    Haematology and biochemistry run on our own analysers, so most bloods are back within the hour and we phone you the same day.

  • Puppy and kitten starter care

    Vaccinations, microchipping, worming and a feeding plan for the first twelve weeks, written up on one card you can keep on the fridge.

  • Dental checks and scale

    A charted check at every visit for dogs and cats, with a printed plan showing what we saw and when we will look again.

  • Microchip scanned Weight and temperature Vaccine history

    Rescue and rehoming checks

    Pre-adoption health checks and paperwork for Wensum Valley Rescue and other partner groups, at cost, booked as a block.

How a first visit works

  1. 08:02You book by phone

    Book by phone or online

    Give us your pet’s name, age and what is worrying you. If you are not sure it is urgent, ring us first and we will tell you.

  2. 16:10You arrive and we weigh in

    Arrive five minutes early

    Dogs come in through the front door on a lead. Cats go straight to the cat-only room. We weigh your pet and take a short history before the vet comes in.

  3. 17:05You leave with results

    Go home with the results

    We run bloods on site and go through every number with you before you leave. If anything needs chasing, we phone you that same day.

Questions owners ask us most

Still unsure? Ring the front desk and a nurse will talk it through.

Walk-ins are for emergencies only. Ring 01603 555 0100 or book online, and if we have a slot free we will see your pet the same day.

Most haematology and biochemistry results are back within an hour. Cultures and biopsies go to an outside laboratory and take three to five working days.

Please book two slots. Cats are seen in the cat-only room and dogs at the front, and we will stagger the times so they never share a corridor.

We keep patients until 7pm on weekdays and 1pm on Saturdays. After that, our out-of-hours partner, Heartline Norfolk, looks after them overnight, and we send your pet’s notes ahead.

We take all the major pet insurers and direct claims for you. For larger bills we offer a four-month interest-free plan; ask at the desk before treatment starts.
