Coworking in a Victorian spinning mill · Neepsend, Sheffield

Somewhere to do your best work, with the kettle already on.

Dovecote is a working mill for freelancers, makers and small creative teams: hot desks on the north floor, resident desks with a drawer that is yours, lockable studios, a podcast booth, a darkroom and a members' kitchen that never runs out of tea.

The spaces

Six ways to work at Dovecote, under one roof

Most members start with a desk and end up using the darkroom on a Thursday. Everything here is shared, and everything is bookable from the same member app.

  • Hot desks

    Turn up, take any free desk on the north floor and get on with it. Day passes are from £22, and you can keep a desk for the morning.

    Mon–Fri · from £22 a day

  • Resident desks

    Your own desk, a lockable drawer and a key to the side door. Settled in for the month, with a business address and post sorted for you.

    Monthly · key and drawer included

  • Private studios

    Lockable rooms from 8 to 22 m² with north light and a door that actually shuts. Nine in the mill, and the big ones take a dozen people.

    Nine studios · keys from day one

  • Podcast booth

    A treated booth with two mics, a pair of headphone amps and a booking calendar that cannot double-book a Tuesday recording.

    Bookable by the hour · edit suite next door

  • Darkroom

    Two wet bays, a red safelight and a drying rack. Induction takes forty minutes, and Tuesday evenings are set aside for beginners.

    Film & process · induction required

  • Members' kitchen

    Fresh tea and filter coffee all day, a shared fridge with a labelled shelf for everyone, and a long table that becomes the mill's lunch club.

    Open whenever the mill is

Membership

How membership works, in three steps

  1. 01

    Book a 20-minute tour

    Walk the mill with a member who has been here for a while. Bring the question you were too embarrassed to ask about the printer.

  2. 02

    Pick your space and start date

    Choose a day pass, a resident desk or a studio. We will hold it for you for a week while you decide, at no cost.

  3. 03

    Collect your fob and your first week

    Your fob works from 6:30 every morning. Your first week is on us, and Maeve will introduce you to the kiln crew on Friday.
