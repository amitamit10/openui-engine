What we do

Everything a year of books needs, in one place.

Six things we handle every month, so the only thing you have to do is photograph receipts and answer the odd question.

  • Monthly books, closed by working day five

    Every bank and card line categorised, reconciled and checked. You get a month that is finished, not a pile to catch up on.

  • Receipts from your phone, read for you

    Photograph a receipt the moment you pay. We pull out the date, supplier and VAT, then attach it to the right transaction.

  • Bank feeds that match themselves

    Connect up to five accounts. Matches come from your own history, and anything unusual is flagged as a question, never guessed at.

  • VAT returns, drafted for your approval

    Quarterly MTD returns prepared with a one-page summary you read first. Nothing is submitted without your yes.

  • Self-assessment, without the January dread

    Income, expenses, allowances and payments on account gathered into one checked return, with time to spare before 31 January.

  • A human accountant, always named

    One accountant learns your business. You get a plain-English note each month, and questions get a real answer within a working day.

How it works

How your books get done

  1. 1

    Connect

    Link your bank and card accounts and forward receipts to your Ledgerleaf inbox. Setup takes about ten minutes, and we sort the rest.

  2. 2

    We close your month

    Each Monday we categorise, reconcile and chase missing receipts. You are only asked about what we genuinely cannot work out.

  3. 3

    You get a clear answer

    A one-page summary: profit so far, what VAT or tax you owe, and how much to set aside. Your accountant is on the other end of the reply.
