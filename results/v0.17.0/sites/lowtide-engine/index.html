What you get

Everything between the recording and the feed.

Six things we do on every show, whether it is a single pilot or a season of twelve. Each one is there so you do not have to chase it.

  • Recording kits that survive a bad connection

    A preset mic, a foam-lined case and a one-page card with a single button. The kit records locally first, so a dropped call costs your guest nothing.

  • Edited from the transcript, not the waveform

    We cut from the words. You approve the transcript, we follow it, and every change shows up as a line you can check in a minute.

  • Loudness set for phones and car speakers

    Every episode lands at -16 LUFS, the level most phone speakers and car systems expect. We clean the noise floor without flattening the voice.

  • Original themes and under-beds

    Short themes and beds written for your show and licensed for the life of the feed. Nothing borrowed from a library that the next show also uses.

  • Chapters and show notes, drafted

    Chapter markers, a timestamped transcript and a show-note draft in your house tone, delivered with every episode for you to edit rather than rewrite.

  • Release times that fit your comms calendar

    We host the feed or hand it over cleanly, schedule releases around your campaigns, and send a monthly download report for every show.

Process

How an episode gets made

Three stages, each with a named person on our side. The median from final take to live episode is 3.5 working days.

  1. 01

    Day 1

    Brief and post the kit

    A forty-minute call to agree the format, the guest list and what a good episode sounds like for your audience. We then post a recording kit to each guest with a booking link.

  2. 02

    Days 2 to 3

    Record, then cut from the transcript

    Guests record from home on the kit. We transcribe within hours of the file arriving, and the edit is built from that transcript. You get a first cut to approve, with notes on anything we were unsure about.

  3. 03

    Day 4

    Score, master and publish

    We add the theme and beds, master to -16 LUFS and check the file on three devices. Chapters and show notes go in with the episode, and it is released on the time you chose.
