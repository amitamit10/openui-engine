Coworking in a Victorian tool-steel mill, Sheffield

Work from a mill that has never stopped making things.

Dovecote Studios is a members' workspace on the top two floors of Tanner Street Mill. Hot desks and resident desks, lockable studios you can close the door on, a podcast booth, a darkroom and a kitchen where the kettle is never empty.

01 / Spaces

Six kinds of room, one building with a lot of character.

The mill was built in 1868 to make files and tool steel. Its windows are still the original cast-iron frames, and the rooms we added are fitted out for the work people actually do here.

  • Hot desks

    Thirty-eight desks in the north bay, bookable by the day or by the month. Take the one by the window with the steel bench, or stand at the high table if you need to move.

  • Resident desks

    Your name on a brass plate, a drawer with a key, and the same spot every week. Twelve resident desks sit along the south wall in the quiet bay.

  • Lockable studios

    Nine studios from 9 to 22 square metres, each with a key that is yours for the booking. Close the door and the mill's clatter disappears, which is why illustrators and sound designers ask for Studio 4.

  • Podcast booth

    A treated room with two dynamic mics, a boom arm, and a door that seals. Book it in two-hour blocks. The cupboard has spare cables and a box of biscuits for guests.

  • Darkroom

    Two enlargers, a wet bench, a safelight and a sink that runs cold and warm. Film and paper are sold at the front desk, and Thursday evenings are for the print group.

  • Members' kitchen

    Proper kettle, filter coffee, a fridge with shelves that belong to whoever labelled them, and a long table for lunch. Tea is refilled from the mill's own filtered tap at 7am, 12 noon and 4pm.

02 / Membership

Three steps from a tour to a key in your hand.

  1. 1

    Come and see the mill

    Book a 30-minute tour with the front desk. We take you past the hot desks, into the studio you're interested in, and down to the darkroom if you're curious.

  2. 2

    Choose your space

    Pick a hot desk, a resident desk or a studio. We'll hold it for up to seven days while you decide, and confirm your start date by email the same afternoon.

  3. 3

    Get your keys and settle in

    Collect your fob at the front desk, sign into the Wi-Fi, and meet the members on the kitchen table. Your first coffee is on us.
