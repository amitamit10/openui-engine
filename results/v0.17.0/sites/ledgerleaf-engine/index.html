What we do

Everything your books need, in one place

You keep trading. We keep the ledger honest, every month, and tell you plainly where you stand.

  • Monthly books, closed by working day five

    Every bank line is matched to an invoice or receipt, categorised, and signed off. You get a month-end summary before the rest of your month starts.

  • Receipts captured from a photo

    Snap the till slip on the train or forward the email receipt. We read the date, supplier and VAT, then file it against the right transaction.

  • VAT returns prepared and submitted

    We reconcile your quarter, check the flat-rate or standard scheme is still right for you, and file through MTD so the figure never comes as a shock.

  • Self-assessment ready before January

    Your figures, allowances and pension contributions are gathered in spring, reviewed with you in the summer, and filed well before 31 January.

  • Bank feeds matched, every week

    We connect your business accounts and cards, then match each payment to the invoice it settles. Anything unmatched is flagged, not guessed at.

  • A named accountant who answers

    You get one accountant who knows your trade. Questions are answered within one working day, by a qualified person, not a ticket queue.

How it works

How your books get done

Three steps, then a steady monthly rhythm that takes about twenty minutes of your time.

  1. 1

    Connect your accounts

    Link your business bank accounts and card in about ten minutes. We send a short list of anything we need from your last year's records, and nothing more.

  2. 2

    Snap receipts as you go

    Photograph receipts or forward the emailed ones. Missing items show up in your app as a single list, so you can answer them at the end of the week.

  3. 3

    Review your month, then move on

    By working day five your books are closed. You get a one-page summary, one question from your accountant if there is one, and the next month's to-do list.
