What every package includes

Six things, quoted up front. Nothing is an extra you find on the day.

Every Brightwater install is sold as a complete package. The roof survey, scaffold decision, mounting, cabling, certificates and monitoring are all in the price you are given in writing.

Free roof survey, in person

A surveyor walks the roof, measures pitch and orientation, checks shade from trees and chimneys, and confirms rafter spacing before anyone quotes you a price.

One fixed quote

A single price, in writing, valid for 30 days. No scaffold surcharge we did not mention at survey.

Roof-safe mounting

We fix to rafters, never to tiles alone, and send a load report with your paperwork so you know exactly what your roof is carrying.

Battery storage

Store the midday surplus for the evening meal and the morning kettle. Five or ten kilowatt-hours, with the cabinet fixed to a sound wall.

Seven-year monitored guarantee

Every system reports every five minutes. If output dips, we call you, and we visit within ten working days.

Ground and barn arrays for farms

Ground or barn mounts for up to twenty panels, with three-phase connection where the supply allows. A separate sub-meter tracks the pump, the grain dryer or the milk cooler on its own bill line.

Installation

How an installation goes, in three steps

  1. 01

    Survey and fixed quote

    A surveyor visits, checks the structure and the shading, and measures the meter position. You receive one fixed price in writing within three working days of the visit.

    Usually 1 visit, about 90 minutes

  2. 02

    Install in one or two days

    A crew of two fits the array and cabling. Most roofs are done without scaffolding. Before we leave, you get a photo log of every cable route and fixing point.

    Most homes: 1 day. Farms: 2 days

  3. 03

    Switch-on and monitoring

    We send the grid notification and the export meter paperwork for you. Your monitoring app goes live the day the system switches on, and the seven-year guarantee starts.

    Average 23 days from survey to switch-on

Questions

What people ask before the survey

Still unsure? Ring us on 01392 000 000 and a surveyor will answer.

Thirty days from the date of the survey. The price changes only if you ask for something different, such as a larger battery or a second array. We never add charges for work we found but did not mention.

Each bracket is fixed through the tiles into the rafters, not into battens alone. We check rafter spacing and size at survey, and we send you a load report showing the weight your roof will carry with the array on it.

Most single-storey and many two-storey homes are done from a ladder and a mobile access tower. We put up scaffold only where the eaves or the access route make it the safer option, and we tell you before you pay.

Every system is monitored every five minutes for seven years. If output falls below what the survey predicted, our team calls you and visits within ten working days to find and fix the fault, at no cost to you.

Yes, if your inverter is hybrid-ready. The Starter Array has a hybrid-ready inverter, so a battery can be added later. We check the inverter model, the cable runs and the spare fuse capacity at survey.

Most houses fall under permitted development. Listed buildings and homes in conservation areas need more checks, and we tell you at survey which route applies. We prepare the paperwork and the grid notification for you.
