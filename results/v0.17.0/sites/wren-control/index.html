What we do

Everything a worried owner needs, under one roof.

Six things we are known for, and the reasons they matter when your pet has gone off their food.

  • Same-day appointments

    Ring by 10am and we will find you a slot that day. Emergencies never wait behind a booking, and we will tell you straight if your pet can wait until tomorrow.

  • A cat-only waiting room

    Cats wait behind a closed door, away from barking dogs, with covered carriers, a quiet corner and a nurse who knows which cat is which.

  • In-house lab, results in about an hour

    Blood, urine and skin samples run on our own analyser while you wait, so the plan is decided in one visit instead of after a week of phone tag.

  • Puppy and kitten clinic

    A twenty-minute first check designed around nervous young animals: vaccines, microchipping, worming and a plain-English feeding and training note.

  • Dental care you can understand

    Scale and polish under anaesthetic, with pre-op bloods first and a written note with photos of what we found and what happens next.

  • Senior and palliative care

    Gentle mobility checks, pain plans reviewed every month, and honest conversations about quality of life at whatever stage you are at.

Your first time with us

How a first visit works

Most first visits take about forty minutes from the moment you walk in. Here is what happens, in order.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your pet

    Call or use the booking form. We ask for species, age, what has changed and any medicines they take. It takes about two minutes, and you can add a photo of the problem.

  2. 2

    Meet a vet who listens

    A twenty-minute consultation, with a nurse to weigh your pet and settle them. We explain each option and its cost before anything is done, and nothing is charged for a quote.

  3. 3

    Leave with a written plan

    You get a printed summary, medicine labels you can read in bad light, and a reminder for the next check. Lab results are texted to you the moment they are ready.
