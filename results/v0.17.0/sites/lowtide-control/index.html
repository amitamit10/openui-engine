What we make

Everything between the first call and the feed.

Six services come with every show we take on. The pricing table says what they cost; this is what arrives.

  • Remote kits, posted to the guest

    A dual-mic kit in a foam case with a one-page guide and a return label, posted within 48 hours of booking. We run a 15-minute level check on the call before anyone presses record. Backup audio records to the kit’s SD card.

  • Cutting to the point, not to the bone

    We cut for pace and leave breaths in unless they distract. You get a timed rough cut within five working days and a note for every edit we made, with the reason beside it.

  • An original theme, written for the show

    Theme, stingers and beds written or licensed for your show, in a key that does not fight your host. Stems come with the master, so the theme can be re-cut for a trailer or a social clip.

  • Feeds that behave on the day

    We host the feed or deliver into yours, submit to the major directories and schedule each drop against your calendar. Downloads are reported from the feed itself, so the numbers in your board pack match the numbers on the platform.

  • Transcripts and show notes

    Timestamped transcripts, chapter markers and show notes drafted from the episode, for accessibility and search. A person reads every one before it goes anywhere, and the transcript ships as a file you own.

  • Sponsor reads, slotted and logged

    We place sponsor reads at natural breaks, record them separately so a sponsor can be swapped without a re-edit, and keep a log of every slot, time and read for your sales team to check against the invoice.

How an episode gets made

Three steps. About three weeks from brief to feed.

Most of the waiting happens while a guest is travelling, which is why we start the kit before we start the edit.

  1. 01

    Brief and booking

    You send a short brief. We send back a two-page session sheet with the guests, the running order and the three questions the episode must answer. Kits go out within 48 hours of booking.

    Hands you: session sheet

  2. 02

    Record and cut

    Remote or in our Bristol studio. Levels are checked in the first two minutes of every track, then we cut a rough version within five working days. You receive timed notes, not a folder of unlabelled files.

    Hands you: timed rough cut and notes

  3. 03

    Score, master and publish

    Theme, bed and stingers added, then mastered to −16 LUFS for podcast platforms. We publish on the date you choose, which on average is 3.5 working days after you approve the master.

    Hands you: master, stems and feed
