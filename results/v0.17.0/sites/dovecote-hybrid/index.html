Spaces

Six rooms, one mill, and nothing you have to carry up the stairs twice.

Everything at Dovecote is booked by the day, the month or the hour, so you pay for the room you use and nothing more.

  • Hot desks

    Sit wherever the light is. Thirty-two hot desks on the north-light floor, bookable by the day or the month, each with a second screen and a lamp that works.

    32 desks · from £24 a day

  • Resident desks

    A bay that stays yours. Each has a locking pedestal drawer, your name on the nameplate and a mailbox for post that actually arrives on time.

    24 bays · from £262 a month

  • Private studios

    Four lockable rooms for one to four people, with sound-treated walls, a door that shuts properly and keyfob access at any hour for late deadlines.

    4 studios · from £476 a month

  • Podcast booth

    A treated booth with two microphones, a small mixing desk and a sofa for guests. Recording sessions are booked in half-hour blocks, so no one waits.

    Booked by the half hour

  • Darkroom

    A working black-and-white darkroom with two enlargers, a wet bench and red safelight. A thirty-minute induction comes before your first session.

    2 enlargers · induction required

  • Members' kitchen

    A long table, proper milk and a tea shelf restocked every morning. The Friday cake is the reason half the building comes in before nine.

    Open 7am to 11pm, seven days

Membership

How membership works, in three steps

Most people are settled in within a week. You never need a contract to come and look.

  1. 1

    Walk the floor

    Book a 40-minute tour with Maren or Tomasz at the front desk. We show you the darkroom and the booth too, even if you only came for a desk.

    40 minutes at 10am, 2pm or 5pm

  2. 2

    Choose your room

    A hot desk, a resident bay or one of the four studios. Moving between them is free in your first 30 days, and a resident bay can be paused for up to two months.

    Up to 30 days free to change your mind

  3. 3

    Collect your fob

    Your fob and kitchen key are ready within one working day. Bring your laptop; the Tuesday breakfast rota and the wifi password are on the wall by the lift.

    1 working day from signing to your first morning

Questions

What the front desk gets asked most

Yes. Day passes are £24 and bookable in the members' app or at the front desk. They cover any hot desk that is free that day, from 8am to 8pm.

Resident and studio members get a fob for the street door and their own room, both working around the clock. Day Desk fobs open the street door from 7am to 11pm.

Monthly memberships roll and need 30 days' notice to end. Annual plans are billed up front and fixed for twelve months, which is what most resident members choose.

Both are booked by the hour through the members' app. Resident and studio members use them free for their included hours; Day Desk members pay £8 an hour. The darkroom needs a thirty-minute induction first, usually on a Thursday evening.

There is no car park on site. Street parking on the roads around the mill is free after 6pm. A secure store in the lower yard holds 40 bikes, and every member gets a space.

Clients are welcome in the hall and the kitchen, and studio members get two guest passes a month. Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the ground floor, but not in the darkroom or the studios.
