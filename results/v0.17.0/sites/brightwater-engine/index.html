What every system includes

Six things we do on every install, whatever the roof

Most installers quote a number and leave you to find the gaps. Here is what you get as standard.

  • Roof-safe mounting

    Rails fix to the rafters, not the tiles. Every fixing is torque-checked and photographed, and the roof is left watertight.

  • A fixed price, in writing

    The figure on your quote is the figure you pay. No scaffold charge, no extra cable run and no surprise consumer-unit upgrade.

  • Battery that fits your day

    Store midday sun for the evening meal, the milking parlour or the tenant's heating. Batteries from 5 to 10 kWh, wall-mounted in a garage or utility room.

  • Seven years, watched

    Each system reports every five minutes. If output falls short for three sunny days, an engineer visits at our cost, not yours.

  • One app for every roof

    Landlords with several properties see each system on one screen, with a message the day output dips and a plain-English monthly summary.

  • Built for farms and multi-roof sites

    Three-phase inverters, a separate meter for each barn or block, and a survey that checks the distribution board before anything is fitted.

Installation

How an installation goes

Three visits, one named contact, and nothing starts until you have signed a fixed quote.

  1. 1

    Survey on day one

    A surveyor spends about 90 minutes on your roof, measures shade from trees and chimneys, and checks your meter and consumer unit. The fixed written quote follows within 48 hours.

  2. 2

    Fitted in one or two days

    A two-person crew fixes the rails, panels and inverter, adds the battery if you chose one, and sends a photo log at the end of each day.

  3. 3

    Switched on and registered

    We notify your network operator, commission the system to MCS standard and set up your app. The seven-year guarantee starts on the day it switches on.
