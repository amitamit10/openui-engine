What we make

Six jobs we do for every show, and one we refuse to skip.

Most production houses bill the recording and leave the rest to you. We take on the parts that usually slip: guest audio, the legal read, the feed set-up and the paper trail.

Remote recording kits

A dynamic mic, a small interface and a pre-addressed return box, posted within 48 hours. We call the guest, line-check them and stay on the line until the first answer is clean.

Editing that keeps the breath

We cut for meaning and leave the human pauses in. Every removal is logged with a timecode, so you can hear what changed and why before you approve the draft.

Scoring and music beds

Original beds and stings written in our room, or library music cleared for your licence. Nothing generated, nothing unlicensed, and the bed is mixed to duck under the host.

Feed set-up and publishing

We build the RSS feed, artwork and metadata for each show, then publish to four directories on a morning you agree. You get the feed link, not a login to someone else’s platform.

Transcripts and show notes

Timestamped transcripts and notes, checked by a person against the audio, so the search on your site finds the moment a guest said the thing you quote.

Safeguarding and legal read, on every script

Every charity and publisher script gets a read for named individuals, factual claims and consent. If a claim has no source, the episode waits in the log until someone answers it. We would rather be late by a day than correct a feed for a month.

Process

How an episode gets made

Ten working days from the planning call to a live episode, with a log at every step.

Brief and kit 5 days
Record and edit 3 days
Score, check, publish 2 days
Day 0Day 5Day 10
  1. 01

    Days 0 to 5

    Brief and kit

    We agree the cut, the guest list and the date on a 30-minute call. If a guest needs a kit, it leaves Bristol within 48 hours with a tracked label and a line-check booked.

  2. 02

    Days 5 to 8

    Record and edit

    Hosts record in Studio B, guests from their kits. We send a first-pass cut within two working days of the session, with the edit log alongside, so every change is one you can question.

  3. 03

    Days 8 to 10

    Score, check, publish

    The bed is written or cleared, the legal read is signed off, and the master goes to your feeds on the morning you chose. You keep the masters, the log and the transcript.
