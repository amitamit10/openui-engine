Moss & Lantern · PC and Switch · Spring 2027

Light the lighthouse the sea forgot.

Moss & Lantern is a cosy puzzle game about Nettle Rock, an island whose lighthouse went dark forty winters ago. Turn the rusted lens rings, time the tides that open the causeway, and carry the lamp back up one hand-drawn room at a time.

The game

Six things that make Nettle Rock feel lived in

Every mechanic grows out of the island itself: the weather, the birds and the slow work of getting a lamp to shine again.

  • Rings that turn

    Rotate three rusted lens rings until the beams line up with the glass. Each ring hides a different hinge, so the same ring can open two rooms.

  • Tides that open paths

    The causeway floods and drains on a readable tide table. Plan a crossing by the chart, then wait out the water if you must. Nothing ever times out.

  • Birds that nest in your rooms

    Gulls and puffins move into rooms you have restored. Their nests change the puzzles nearby, so a finished room is never quite finished.

  • A logbook in your own hand

    Every keeper writes in the logbook. Sketch the rooms, pin notes to the tide and trace the birds. Export pages as the game's own postcards.

  • A score that follows the weather

    Fourteen cues by the composer Iwa Dunmore swell with wind and fade with fog. The soundtrack changes with the room you are in, not the level you have reached.

  • Hints that never shout

    Hold the lantern button for two seconds and the nearest thing that matters glows. The lantern shows where to look, never the answer itself.

Demo

How a night on the island plays

A full night takes about forty minutes in the demo and two to three hours in the game. Here is the shape of one.

A typical night in the demo, in minutes (40 in total)

  1. 01

    Dusk

    Read the island

    Walk the shore with the logbook open. Note which causeway stones are wet, which rooms the gulls have claimed and where the lens rings are stuck.

    9 min
  2. 02

    Night

    Turn the lens

    Climb the stair, one room at a time. Line up the three rings against the storm glass until the beam finally sweeps across the water.

    24 min
  3. 03

    Dawn

    Open the log

    Draw what you restored into the logbook. The island answers in the morning: a new bird, a path that was not there, a song you can hum.

    7 min

The demo covers the shore, the first two lens rings and the cottage.
