What we install

Six parts of a system, each designed for the roof it sits on.

Every Brightwater job starts with the roof, not a catalogue. These are the parts you will actually see on your property and the reasons we choose them.

  • Roof-safe mounting

    Stainless hooks sit under the tile line and are sealed at every fixing. We check the batten layout before drilling and leave you a photo log of each one, so you know where everything went.

  • Battery storage

    Lithium batteries from 5 to 15 kWh, sized to your evening use and fitted in the garage, utility room or barn. Stored sun for the hours after tea.

  • Monitored for seven years

    Every system reports every five minutes. If an inverter faults or a string drops off, our engineers see it on the dashboard before you notice the meter.

  • Farm and outbuilding arrays

    Ground mounts and barn roofs for milking parlours, grain stores and irrigation pumps. Three-phase inverters are standard on our Farm Array, with a named engineer for your site.

  • Ready for an electric car

    Charge from your own roof and send the surplus to the grid on your export tariff. Family and Farm installs include EV charger wiring, so the cable run is done once, not twice.

  • A fixed quote, written down

    The price on your written quote is the price on your invoice. There is no surcharge for scaffolding, a patch of batten that needs replacing or a consumer unit upgrade we spotted during the survey. If we find a defect that makes the job unsafe, we show you photos first and do nothing without your written agreement.

How it works

How an installation goes, in three steps.

From survey to switch-on averages 23 days. Most of that is the network operator approving the connection, which we chase for you.

  1. Days 1 to 2

    Survey and fixed quote

    A surveyor walks the roof with you, measures pitch, orientation and shading, and checks your consumer unit. The written quote arrives within 48 hours and the price does not move.

  2. Days 3 to 5

    Mounting and wiring

    A two-person crew fits rails and panels with sealed roof hooks, then wires the inverter and battery. Scaffold goes up only where access needs it. Most homes take two to three working days.

  3. Days 6 to 23

    Approval, switch-on and monitoring

    We notify your network operator, which usually takes about two weeks. Then we commission the system while you watch and connect your app. The seven-year guarantee starts on the day it is switched on.
