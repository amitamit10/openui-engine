What we do

Everything your year needs, handled in the background.

Six things we take off your desk, each one done by a person who checks the numbers before you see them.

  • Monthly books, closed on time

    Bank lines matched and categorised, then closed by the seventh working day. You see the profit figure before you need it.

  • Receipt capture

    Photograph a receipt or forward the email. We read the supplier, date and amount, then attach it to the right line.

  • VAT returns, checked line by line

    Your quarterly return drafted from the ledger, with a reconciliation you can read before anything goes to HMRC.

  • Self Assessment, ready in January

    Income, allowable expenses and capital allowances gathered into one pack, so your return is a review, not a scramble.

  • One question at a time

    When something is unclear, we ask in plain English and suggest an answer you can accept with a single tap.

  • A named accountant, every month

    Priya, Tom or Amara, the same person each month. They reply within one working day and call you when a number looks wrong.

How it works

How your books get done

Three steps, and the first one takes about ten minutes.

  1. 1

    Connect and forward

    Link your business bank account through open banking and forward receipts to your Ledgerleaf inbox. We check the first month with you on a 15-minute call.

  2. 2

    We close the month

    By the seventh working day every line is matched, categorised and reconciled. Anything unclear reaches you as one question, not a pile of them.

  3. 3

    Your accountant signs off

    A named accountant reviews the month. You get a one-page summary of profit, the tax to set aside and the next thing to do.
