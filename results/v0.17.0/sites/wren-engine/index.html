What we do under one roof

Everything from a first injection to a hard decision, handled calmly.

Our services are built around how owners actually feel when they bring a pet in. Each one has a clear next step.

  • Same-day appointments

    Most days we hold back slots for calls made that morning. If your pet is unwell today, ring at 8am and we will find you a time before lunch.

  • A cat-only waiting room

    Cats wait in their own quiet room with covered carriers and raised perches, away from dogs. Ask for the cat room when you book and we will make sure it is ready.

  • In-house lab results in an hour

    Blood and urine tests run on our own analyser, so the result comes back while you wait or within the hour. You get a plain-English summary, not a sheet of codes.

  • Puppy and kitten starter care

    Vaccines, microchipping, worming and a weight plan matched to how fast your puppy or kitten grows. First-time owners get a written calendar and a nurse call the week after each visit.

  • Senior pet and heart checks

    For pets over eight we check blood pressure, heart rhythm, joints and teeth, then plan the next six months together. Medicines are dispensed at the clinic with the dose written on the label.

  • Emergencies: call first

    Collapse, a bloated or hard belly, bleeding that will not stop, or breathing with an open mouth: ring 01603 555 142 straight away. We triage by phone and tell you whether to come in or go to the emergency hospital.

How a first visit works

Three steps, and you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Tell us what has changed

    Book online or call. We ask three things: your pet's species and age, what you have noticed, and whether they are eating and drinking. That decides which slot we offer.

  2. 2

    Arrive ten minutes early

    A nurse weighs your pet and takes a short history in the consulting room. Cats go straight to the cat-only waiting room, so they do not sit next to a barking dog.

  3. 3

    Leave with a written plan

    You get a summary of what we found, the options with prices before anything is done, and the date to come back. Lab results are texted to you the moment they are ready.
