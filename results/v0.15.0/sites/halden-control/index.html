01 — The workWhat we take on

Six kinds of project, one studio, and a fee that is agreed up front.

We take on a small number of jobs at a time so that every project keeps the same architect from first site visit to planning decision.

  • Design stage

    A fixed fee for RIBA stages 2 to 4

    The price for concept, developed design and planning drawings is written into the appointment. Hourly timesheets are not part of our contract, so changes to the brief are quoted before we draw them.

  • Fabric first

    Wall build-ups we model before we draw

    Every external wall, roof and floor is modelled in-house against Part L and our own low-energy target. On most of our homes the external walls reach 0.13 W/m²K with insulation placed inside the solid wall.

  • In-house engineering

    A structural engineer in the studio

    Ibrahim Osei sizes beams, lintels and steel for every job in the same room as the architect. Clashes with services are found while the plan is still moving, not after the tender has gone out.

  • Planning

    Planning submission in nine weeks

    The average from first brief to a validated planning application across our last forty projects. We prepare heritage statements, ecology scoping and the drawing set together, so the council receives one complete application.

  • Barn conversions

    Historic frames, rural policy, bat surveys

    We start with the frame, the roof and the protected species survey, then plan the rooms around what the building can take. Our conversions in the Dales and Calder valley have been approved on the first submission in nine out of ten cases.

  • School extensions

    Phased builds that keep classrooms open

    Working with four academy trusts across West Yorkshire, we phase extensions around the timetable and the Ofsted calendar. Sawtooth rooflights bring north light into the teaching spaces without overheating in June.

02 — The processThree stages

How a project runs, from first visit to planning.

  1. 01 Weeks 0 to 2

    Site visit and written brief

    We walk the site with you, measure the existing building and check the planning history. Within ten working days you receive the brief and a fixed fee in writing.

    • Measured survey and photo record
    • Planning constraints note
    • Written fee and programme
  2. 02 Weeks 2 to 9

    Design stage, fixed fee

    Concept, developed design and planning drawings. You approve at three named gates, and nothing is drawn twice because each gate is checked against the energy model.

    • Gate A: concept and massing
    • Gate B: developed design and energy model
    • Gate C: planning drawing set
  3. 03 Week 9 onward

    Submission, then tender

    We submit to Leeds City Council or your local authority and manage questions from the case officer. While the application is decided, we prepare tender packs for the contractors you choose.

    • Validated planning application
    • Tender pack and specification
    • Building control liaison on full service

06 — QuestionsFrequently asked

Before the site visit.

Yes. The fee for RIBA stages 2 to 4 is written into the appointment before we start. We only revise it if the brief changes after Gate A, and any change is quoted in writing before we do the work.

Planning application fees, surveys we commission on your behalf such as topographical, bat and drainage surveys, and VAT. Each of these is listed at the site visit with an estimate.

Full service includes liaison with building control from the first inspection to the completion certificate. On design stage appointments we prepare the Part B and Part L information so that your chosen inspector has everything at the start.

We design to a Passivhaus-equivalent fabric standard as a matter of course. Formal Passivhaus certification is optional and is quoted separately, because the certifier needs its own modelling and site-testing fee.

We submit around nine weeks after the first brief. Councils usually decide minor applications within eight weeks of validation, though a case officer can ask for more time, and we handle those questions while you wait.

Most of our work is within an hour of Leeds, across West and North Yorkshire. We take projects further afield when the school trust or client already works with us, and we tell you at the first call if the distance changes the fee.
