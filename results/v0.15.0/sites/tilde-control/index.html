What you get

Three languages, taught the same way: you speak in every lesson.

Pick Portuguese for the bakery and the in-laws, Polish for the landlord and the tram, or Japanese for the train to Osaka. Every course uses the same six-person classes and the same Thursday speaking club.

  • Português

    A Brazilian or European track, starting with the café, the pharmacy and the phrase “I think I have a cold.”

  • Polski

    Cases arrive in week one with a cheat sheet, not a panic. You will say “dziękuję” correctly by week three.

  • 日本語

    Kana from the first Tuesday, then the phrases you need on a train, in a shop and at the izakaya after work.

  • Classes of six, never seven

    Small enough that nobody hides at the back. In a 50-minute lesson each learner talks for four to six minutes, and the teacher keeps the tally.

  • A speaking club every Thursday

    Forty minutes, one topic, no new grammar. Think “What would you cook on a bad day?” with a timer. Missed it? The recording is in your library by morning.

  • A progress report a human wrote

    Every six weeks and at the end of term, your teacher writes a real note: what you can say now, one thing to fix, and one thing to try this week.

  • Sounds that trip you up, drilled

    Polish clusters like “wsz”, Portuguese nasal vowels, Japanese pitch accent. We drill the sounds your first language finds hard, not a generic list.

  • Writing in your own script

    Kana from week one, Polish spelling rules explained once and properly, and the Portuguese accents that stop your messages looking sleepy.

  • Times that fit your life

    Evening and weekend slots across CET, GMT, BRT and JST, and every class recorded for the week when work eats the lesson.

How learning works

From first hello to a whole sentence, in three steps.

  1. 1

    Take a 20-minute placement chat

    A relaxed video call with a teacher. We ask about your week, not your grammar. You leave with a level, a class time and a short list of words you actually need.

  2. 2

    Join a class of six with your teacher

    Your teacher is a native speaker with at least three years in front of a class. Each week brings a new topic and a warm-up round. Nobody reads from a script, including the teacher.

  3. 3

    Speak on Thursdays, then read your report

    Join the speaking club, then receive your progress report after week six and at the end of term. Real notes, with targets you can check in the next lesson.
