The programme

Six strands, one harbour, two days.

Talks run on four parallel tracks. Every session is tagged Technical, Practical or Policy so you can plan around your background.

  • Underwater robotics

    Twelve talks on tether handling, manipulator trials in kelp-choked water and the first honest numbers on autonomy below 60 metres.

  • Seaweed farming

    Longline seeding, harvest timing and the real costs of kelp at scale, from growers who run working farms in the North Sea.

  • Coastal sensing

    Buoys, tide gauges and acoustic arrays: what to deploy, how to service it, and what to do when the next storm takes it.

  • Marine data

    Standards, open datasets and the pipelines that turn raw CTD casts into something a planning officer can actually open on a Monday.

  • Harbour boat session

    Day two, 14:00. Three vessels leave Footdee for a two-hour sensor deployment and kelp survey, after a safety brief on the quay.

  • Six hands-on workshops

    Splicing a mooring line, calibrating a salinity logger, writing a QA script. Each workshop has 24 places, first come, first served.

How attending works

Three steps from ticket to the water.

  1. 01

    Pick a pass

    A Day Pass covers one day. A Full Summit covers both. Team of Five gives five Full Summit passes and a shared workshop block.

  2. 02

    Build your programme

    The programme goes live on 1 August 2027. Choose talks across the four tracks and reserve your workshop seat when you register.

  3. 03

    Get on the water

    Sign up for the harbour boat session at check-in on day one. Lifejackets, the safety brief and a hot drink are included.
