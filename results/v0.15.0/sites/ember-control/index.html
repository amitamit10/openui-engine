The kitchen

Six things we will not cut corners on.

The menu changes with the coast and the market, but these stay on it every week.

  • Oak-fired grill

    Whole bream and mackerel, and a shoulder of lamb, cooked over oak coals raked into a bed beside the pass.

  • Apple-wood smoke

    Duck breast and our cultured butter are smoked over apple wood for forty minutes before they reach the table.

  • Bread from the hearth

    Sourdough and rye, shaped at five and baked in the deck oven. The rye starter has been fed every day since 2017.

  • A short natural-wine list

    Twenty-two bottles, changed monthly, from growers we have visited. Two by the glass are always from Somerset.

  • Eight seats at the fire

    A counter wrapped around the grill. Watch the lamb turned at eight, and ask the cooks anything you like.

  • The 24-seat private room

    A room above the bar with its own pass and a dedicated host, for anniversaries, launches and dinners of 12 to 24.

Your evening

How a visit works

  1. 01

    Book the night

    Reserve online or email us. Tell us about allergies and the occasion, and we will write both on your table card.

  2. 02

    Sit close to the fire

    Have a glass at the bar while your table is set. Your first plate comes off the grill the moment you sit down.

  3. 03

    Take the bread home

    Leave with a loaf of rye, baked that morning and wrapped in brown paper, for breakfast the next day.
