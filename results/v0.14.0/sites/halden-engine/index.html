01 — Projects

Five kinds of project we do well, and how we approach each.

  • Low-energy homes

    Fabric first. We specify walls, roofs and glazing to a U-value of 0.15 W/m²K or better before any kit goes on the roof. Every design is modelled in PHPP and checked against SAP, so the numbers hold at handover.

    Typical programme: 9–12 weeks to planning

  • Structural engineering, in-house

    Our engineer sits in the studio and works in the same model as the architects. Steel, timber and CLT are designed together, so the frame shapes the plan rather than arriving as a late change at tender.

    No separate consultant needed for planning

  • £7,800 Design stage, fixed

    Fixed-fee design stage

    One number, agreed before we start. The design stage has a written scope, three options, one developed scheme and two rounds of revisions. The fee does not drift when the design gets more interesting.

    Scope and fee confirmed in writing

  • Planning submissions

    Drawings that a planning officer can read on the first pass. Red-line plans, heritage and design access statements, and the application forms are produced by the team that designed the building.

    First-round decisions on most applications

  • Barn conversions

    Keeping the frame and adding the life. We repair historic timber, detail new openings so they read as honest additions, and prepare heritage evidence before the design is fixed rather than after.

    Common in the Dales and the Pennine edge

  • School extensions

    Classrooms with even daylight and no glare. North-light rooflights, phased works that keep the school open, and site rules on noise and deliveries agreed with the head before the first drawing.

    Designed to the trust brief where one exists

02 — Process

How a project runs, in three steps.

Most briefs reach planning submission in eleven weeks. Each step ends with a decision you make, not a deadline we set.

  1. 01

    Brief and site visit

    We measure, photograph and test the site with you, then write a one-page brief that sets the budget ceiling and the energy target. You approve the brief before we draw anything.

    Weeks 1–2
  2. 02

    Design stage

    Three options, each with cost and energy figures. You choose one, and we develop it to planning-ready drawings, with structural and energy calculations inside the fixed fee.

    Weeks 3–9
  3. 03

    Planning submission

    We submit the application and answer the first round of officer queries ourselves. On full service we carry straight on to a tender pack and contractor shortlist.

    Weeks 10–11
