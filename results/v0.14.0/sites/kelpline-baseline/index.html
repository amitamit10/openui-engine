Aberdeen · 12–13 October 2027

Ocean technology, tested in cold water.

Kelpline Summit brings underwater robotics, seaweed farming, coastal sensing and marine data into one two-day conference on Aberdeen’s harbour. Forty talks, six hands-on workshops and a boat trip out to a live sensor buoy, led by the people who run the kit.

The programme

Two days, three tracks, and one harbour.

Talks cover Robotics, Farming and Sensing. Workshops use real kit, so you leave with a working prototype, a cleaned dataset or a plan for your own site.

  • Robotics · Farming · Sensing

    Forty talks from people in the water

    Field reports from operators of ROVs, kelp longlines and tide gauges. Each talk runs 25 minutes with ten left for questions from the floor.

  • Six workshops · up to 24 seats

    Hands-on sessions with real kit

    Half-day workshops on ROV piloting, kelp line tension modelling, low-cost sonar and quality checks for coastal sensors.

  • Thursday afternoon · 90 min

    Harbour boat session

    Two boats leave Aberdeen Harbour to see a deployed sensor buoy and a small ROV in use. Life jackets are fitted on the quay.

  • 30-minute slots

    Marine data clinic

    Bring a survey or sensor dataset and spend half an hour with a data engineer from our partner labs on cleaning and QA.

  • Six seed-stage teams

    Founder pitch dock

    Five minutes on the main stage, then a feedback session with procurement leads from coastal authorities and harbour trusts.

  • Closed session · by invitation

    Coastal authority roundtable

    Councils and harbour trusts compare permitting timelines and agree how to share monitoring data without duplicating survey work.

Attending

How attending works

Three steps from ticket to the quayside. Most delegates finish step two in under ten minutes.

  1. 1

    Pick your pass

    Choose a Day Pass, a Full Summit or a Team of Five. Every pass includes lunch on the day and the Summit app for scheduling and messaging.

  2. 2

    Build your programme

    Save talks and choose a first and second workshop. Workshop seats are allocated by ballot on 1 September 2027, and you will hear by 8 September.

  3. 3

    Collect your badge

    Badges are issued from 08:00 each morning at the venue. Show the QR code from your confirmation email and you are in.

The Net Loft, Footdee

A former net store on Aberdeen’s harbour front, a 15-minute walk from the railway station. Talks run on the ground floor, which is step-free throughout, and the quiet room is on the first floor.

Parking on Footdee is limited, so the bus and rail are the easiest options. Accessible parking bays can be booked when you register.
