Work and what comes with it

Six kinds of pictures, one calm way of making them.

Every commission starts with a walk through the space and ends with a gallery you can actually use. This is what sits inside a booking.

  • Window-light sessions

    We plan around the sun's path through your rooms. A 40-minute pre-scout maps where the light falls at nine, noon and five, and the shot list follows it.

  • Film and digital, side by side

    Medium-format 6×7 and 35 mm film, with two digital bodies running alongside. Film scans arrive in the same private gallery as the digital files.

  • Timeline planning

    A shot list built with your planner and your timings. On weddings a second shooter joins from the first look, so the ceremony and the room are covered at once.

  • Restrained retouching

    Colour and skin stay close to how they looked in the room. Two rounds of edits are included, and faces and bodies are never reshaped.

  • Prints and print-ready files

    Archival cotton-rag prints for the home, and 300 dpi files with colour profiles agreed before delivery for magazines and menus.

  • Usage licences for hospitality

    Licences for menus, websites and booking platforms, priced per year and written in plain language before the shoot, not after it.

About the studio

How a shoot works, in three steps.

Ines trained in Porto in a film lab and has worked in Lisboa since 2012. She talks less on set than most photographers do, because the best frames usually come after people forget the camera is there.

  1. 01

    Talk and scout

    A 45-minute call, then a walk through the room or the route. We agree which light we want, who needs to be in frame and what can be cut.

  2. 02

    Shoot calmly

    Short, quiet instructions and room to move. Weddings run from a shot list, portraits stay in one location, and commercial work follows your service schedule.

  3. 03

    Edit and deliver

    A first edit arrives within 21 days, followed by two rounds of changes. Film scans and print-ready files go into the same private gallery.
