What you get

Everything a good class needs, and nothing you will not use.

No gamified streaks for their own sake and no classes of forty. Just six people, one teacher and a lot of talking.

Six seats, no hiding

Classes cap at six, so everyone speaks in every lesson. Our average is 5.6, and nobody disappears behind a camera tile.

A speaking club that talks back

Sixty minutes on Thursday evenings with rotating topics: the GP appointment, the landlord’s email, a first date in Kraków.

Pronunciation you can hear

Record a sentence, overlay your waveform on your teacher’s and adjust. Polish consonant clusters get their own drill, because they deserve one.

Reports a human actually wrote

Each month your teacher writes a page on what you can say now, what is next and one habit to stop worrying about.

Timetables that fit real life

Pick a morning, lunchtime or evening slot. Shift pattern changed? Swap into another class with 24 hours’ notice.

Catch up without falling behind

Every class is recorded for a week, with the vocabulary list and a two-minute review quiz to make the missed lesson stick.

How learning works

Three steps from “hello” to the parent’s evening.

  1. 1

    Tell us where you are starting

    A 20-minute level chat with a real teacher, not a quiz bot. We will be honest if you are ready for Core Class or should begin with Club.

  2. 2

    Join your class of six

    Two live sessions a week, plus the Thursday club. Cameras are optional on weeknights. Homework takes about 30 minutes, not two hours.

  3. 3

    Read what you can now say

    Each month a named teacher writes what you can do, what comes next and the one thing that will make the next month easier.
