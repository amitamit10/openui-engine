The offer

Three fires, one short menu.

Everything on the list is cooked over oak, apple or the bread oven. We buy whole animals and whole fish so nothing is wasted and the menu changes with the week's landings and the farm deliveries.

Whole fish over oak

Mackerel, sea bream and John Dory land at Brixham on Tuesdays and go onto the grate whole, skin down, with salsa verde and charred lemon. Ask for it off the bone and we'll do that at the pass.

Aged lamb, apple-wood smoke

Shoulder and rack from a flock on the Mendips, dry-aged for 60 days in our cold room, then finished over apple wood until the fat turns glossy. Served with rye, cooked greens and the pan juices.

Sourdough and rye, baked daily

Our starter has been fed every morning since we opened in 2017. Loaves come out of the bread oven from 6am, and the rye is shaped by hand, so ask for a slice with the butter we churn in Chew Magna.

A short natural-wine list

Twenty-two bottles, changing monthly, mostly from growers in Italy, Slovenia and the Loire. Our team pours a taste before you commit, and there is always one orange wine by the glass.

Vegetables that taste of the fire

Cauliflower roasted in the bread oven with brown butter and hazelnuts, charred leeks with smoked cream, and seasonal greens. Every menu has at least three dishes without meat or fish.

The 24-seat private room

A room with its own hearth-side table for birthdays, wakes, supper clubs and corporate dinners. A set menu is shared across the table, and we can plan the wine list with you in advance.

Visit

How a visit works

Three steps from booking to the last spoon of sorbet. Nothing is rushed and nothing is kept waiting once it leaves the fire.

  1. 01

    Book your time

    Reserve online or ring the bar. We hold tables for 15 minutes past your booking and ask about allergies now, so the grill is planned around you.

  2. 02

    Arrive at the hearth

    Walk past the open fire to the bar. A glass of natural wine or a rye and butter plate is waiting while the table is laid for you.

  3. 03

    Share from the fire

    Dishes arrive as they come off the grill, built for sharing. Order a whole fish for the table or a single lamb rib, and we'll pace the courses for you.
