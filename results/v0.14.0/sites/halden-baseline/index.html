Projects and services

Six kinds of work, one method: fix the fee, model the energy, then draw.

Most of our work is a new low-energy house, a barn or a school extension, but the method is the same. We know what the building must cost to run before we commit to its shape.

  • Low-energy new homes

    Timber-frame and masonry houses detailed to near-passive standard, typically with 60 to 70 per cent less heating demand than a comparable 2010 build. Every project is airtightness-tested on site.

  • Barn conversions

    We have converted 22 agricultural buildings across the Vale of York and the Dales. Roof trusses and frame are surveyed as part of the fixed-fee feasibility stage, so you know the structure before you buy.

  • School extensions

    Classroom and hall extensions planned around term dates and phased so lessons carry on. We have delivered 14 extensions for primary and secondary sites, with the trust and the contractor working from one programme.

  • Feasibility studies

    A fixed-fee appraisal of the site, planning history and budget, with an options report in three weeks. If the scheme does not stack up, we say so in writing and you have spent a fraction of a full design fee.

  • In-house structural engineering

    Our engineer, Sam Pirie, sits in the studio rather than on a retainer across town. Frames, foundations and retrofit loads are checked before the design is frozen, not after the council has seen it.

  • Planning submission

    We prepare the drawings, design and access statement and energy statement, then manage pre-application advice with the local authority. The submission is complete when it leaves the studio.

How a project runs

Three stages, each with a fixed fee and a gate you approve.

  1. 01

    Weeks 1 to 2

    Site visit and brief

    We walk the site, read the planning history and agree in writing what success looks like. The feasibility fee is confirmed within 48 hours of the visit.

  2. 02

    Weeks 3 to 10

    Design stage

    Concept, then developed design, with energy modelling and the structural strategy running in parallel. You sign off three gates before anything goes to the council.

  3. 03

    Weeks 11 to 13

    Planning submission

    We submit complete drawings, statements and energy calculations, then answer the planning officer's questions. Our median from brief to submission is 13 weeks.
