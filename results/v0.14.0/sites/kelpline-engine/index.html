The programme

Six strands, one harbour, two days of straight answers

Every session is pitched at people who build, fund or regulate ocean kit. Expect field logs, failure reports and numbers you can check.

  • Underwater robotics track

    Eleven talks on manipulator arms, hull inspection and fibre-tether power, each with the dive logs behind it.

  • Seaweed farming field clinic

    Line design, seeding timing and harvest logistics from three working farms, plus a Saturday clinic on rope spacing.

  • Coastal sensing and buoys

    Wave, turbidity and salinity sensors that survive a North Sea winter, and how coastal authorities are using the data.

  • Marine data workshops

    Six hands-on sessions on cleaning, versioning and publishing sensor data, using real logs from the Moray Firth.

  • Harbour boat session

    Day two, 36 places. We sail from North Pier to watch a live tether deployment and a buoy recovery, then debrief on deck.

  • Startup pitch table

    Eight founders pitch for four minutes each to a panel of harbour investors and procurement leads, with written feedback.

How attending works

From booking to the boat in three steps

  1. 1

    Pick your pass

    Choose a Day Pass, the Full Summit or a Team of Five. You get a confirmation and a session code within minutes.

  2. 2

    Build your route

    Use the planner to mark the talks and workshops you want. It flags clashes between the two tracks and holds your boat seat.

  3. 3

    Sail, dive and debrief

    Arrive at the Shore Hall for registration. Each evening, the day's speakers and attendees meet at the harbour bar to compare notes.
