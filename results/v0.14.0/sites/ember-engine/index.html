The menu

What comes off the fire

The menu changes with the boats and the orchard. These are the six things we cook most weeks, and the reasons we cook them the way we do.

  • Whole fish, oak-grilled

    Line-caught sea bream and mackerel from Brixham day boats, split and laid skin-side down over oak embers. Finished with salsa verde and charred lemon, served whole for the table to share.

  • Aged lamb shoulder

    Forty-day dry-aged shoulder from a Mendip flock, slow-roasted over apple wood for six hours. Carved at the table with rye crumb, anchovy butter and whatever the orchard is giving us that week.

  • Sourdough and rye, every morning

    Loaves are mixed at five and out of the deck oven by nine. The rye is leavened with a starter we have kept since the day we opened, and the day-old loaves go to the bar as toast.

  • A short natural-wine list

    Twenty-two bottles and eight by the glass, chosen with the kitchen. Chalky whites from Gloucestershire, skin-contact orange from the Loire, and one Jura red that is our answer to the lamb.

  • Apple and oak, sourced close

    Nineteen suppliers within forty miles of the kitchen. Our oak comes from a Somerset farm that prunes its trees in February and sends us the offcuts. Apple wood comes from the same orchards as our cider.

  • The 24-seat private room

    A glass-walled room beside the bar for birthdays, wakes and corporate dinners. Set menus or the full tasting, with one host from the first enquiry to the last glass.

Visiting

How a visit works

Three steps from booking to the last piece of bread. Nothing arrives all at once, because the fire decides the pace.

  1. 1

    Book, or walk in early

    Reserve online or call the house line. We hold four bar seats for walk-ins until 6pm each night, first come, first seated.

  2. 2

    Arrive to the smell of oak

    Start at the bar with a glass and a slice of rye, or go straight to your table. Your server takes you through the fire menu and the day's fish.

  3. 3

    Share the fire, take the bread home

    Dishes come when the grill is ready, not all at once. Leave with a sourdough loaf; we bake extra for the Thursday collection.
