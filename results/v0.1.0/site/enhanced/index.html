How it works

From signal to resolution in three steps

  1. 01

    Connect your signals

    Point Northstar at the monitoring, deploy and chat sources you already run. It learns which signals usually belong together for each service.

  2. 02

    Let Northstar route it

    When an incident is confirmed, the right engineer is paged with context: affected service, recent changes and suggested first checks.

  3. 03

    Review the record

    The timeline is already written as the incident unfolds. Edit the draft postmortem, approve the follow-ups and close the loop in minutes.

Questions, answered

Northstar correlates alerts, error spikes and SLO breaches within a service. When several signals cross their thresholds together, it opens one incident and asks a human to confirm severity. You can tune thresholds per service at any time.

Northstar connects to common chat, metrics, logging, ticketing and deploy tools through native integrations or a standard webhook. If your tool is not listed, a generic webhook and metrics import cover most cases.

Paging follows the on-call schedule for the affected service, including overrides and time-off. If the primary does not acknowledge within your window, the escalation policy moves to the secondary and then to the engineering manager.
