FAQ

Questions, answered

How quickly can we connect our monitoring tools?
Most teams connect their first source in under twenty minutes. Common alerting and metrics integrations are included out of the box.
Does Northstar replace our paging tool?
It can. Northstar includes on-call schedules and escalation policies, or it can sit alongside an existing pager and route through it.
How does the AI decide which engineer to page?
It uses service ownership, schedules and escalation rules. Every routing decision lists the inputs it used, and you can override it at any time.
Is our incident data secure?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. SOC 2 Type II and GDPR documentation are available on request.
Can we edit the AI-drafted postmortems?
Yes. Drafts are a starting point. Your team edits, approves and publishes the final version, and every change is recorded.
What happens if we go over our plan?
We never switch off alerting. We email you before any change to your plan takes effect.
