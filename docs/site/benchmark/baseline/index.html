Features

Everything from first alert to published postmortem, in one place.

Northstar replaces the scramble of channels, spreadsheets and blank documents with a single incident record your team can trust.

Signal detection that groups the noise

Correlated alerts from monitors, logs and deploys merge into one incident, with the most likely cause attached.

Paging that follows ownership

Service ownership and escalation policies route each incident to the right engineer, with automatic fallback if nobody acknowledges.

A live timeline, written for you

Responder messages, deploys and status changes become a timestamped timeline. Nobody has to reconstruct it afterwards.

Postmortem drafts in minutes

Summary, customer impact and contributing factors are drafted from the timeline. Your team reviews and approves before anything is shared.

SLO burn at a glance

Track error budget burn per service and get an early warning before a reliability target is breached.

Runbooks where you need them

Relevant runbooks are suggested from past incidents on the same service, ranked by how often they actually resolved the problem.

How it works

From first alert to published postmortem in three steps.

  1. 1

    Connect your signals

    Point Northstar at your monitors, logs and deploy events. Sensible detection defaults start working on day one and tune to your baseline over the first week.

  2. 2

    Respond with the right person

    Correlated signals open a single incident. Paging follows your service ownership and escalation policy to whoever is on call.

  3. 3

    Learn and publish

    The timeline is recorded as responders work. When the incident closes, a postmortem draft is ready for review within minutes.
